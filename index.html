How to test this

A sample job is already loaded below: a made-up request for a real building, the aquatic centre in Hamilton. Its measurements come from LINZ's free laser scans and aerial photos.

  1. Start the quote in step 1. Paste a text or email (there's an example to try) and Claude reads it the way TradeHire's quote skill does, listing anything still needed. Or type the details in. If you're not signed in to Claude, the paste option is hidden, so type the details.
  2. Speak the job in step 1 instead of typing: tap the microphone, say the address, the job, storeys, weeks, access and the client, then tap Stop. The details fill in and the address is measured. Your browser turns the speech into text (Chrome and Edge use Google's service, Safari uses Apple's).
  3. Measure any address in step 2. Type the number and street, with the suburb or town if you like, press Find and pick the address. The building is measured live from LINZ in a few seconds: outline, each wall's height, roof pitch and the aerial photo.
  4. Mark up the plan in step 2. Pick a colour, then tap walls: dark green for scaffold, highlighter green for shrink wrap, blue for edge protection. Choose Power line and tap two points to draw a red marker. Zoom in for large sites.
  5. Fix the outline if it's off. Choose Adjust outline, drag a corner to move it, tap a wall to add a corner, or select a corner and remove it. Tap any wall to change its height.
  6. Check the price in step 3. Every line uses an AroFlo part. Rates marked To confirm still need TradeHire's figure.
  7. Add site photos at the bottom of step 1, from the customer's email or taken on site.
  8. Finish in step 4. Download the coloured plan, copy the quote, and see exactly what would go to AroFlo. Try the AroFlo buttons: find the client, use the price list, check with AroFlo, approve and put the quote in, then attach the photos. In this demo AroFlo's answers are examples; nothing is saved.
  9. Found something wrong? Note the address and what you expected, and send it to Karrina.
